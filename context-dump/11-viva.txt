11 — VIVA Q&A + LIVE DEMO SCRIPT
================================================================================
Q: Why multi-agent instead of one big prompt? A: Separation makes failures
diagnosable — each stage logs evidence; when our deploy died, the step log
pointed at blob creation, not "the AI". Independent critic + deterministic
scanner beats self-review. Q: Why not auto-deploy everything? A: The risk
gate + scope rule + safety scanner hold the line; S2 money bug and S4
injection both parked instead of shipping. Q: What if the LLM hallucinates?
A: Paths must exist in the snapshot, search blocks must match exactly once,
diffs must be non-empty and within limits — code constraints, not wishes.
Q: What if AI key is missing? A: AI_UNAVAILABLE, issues wait, retry recovers
— demonstrated. Q: How do you know it's live? A: version.json must report
the pushed SHA plus 2xx health probes; else DEPLOY_TIMEOUT or UNVERIFIED —
never assumed. Q: What about prompt injection? A: Untrusted delimiters +
code-enforced gates; our injection fixture forced approval. Q: Scale? A:
Honestly single-instance (in-process queue, one Render instance); quality
follows the model; free tiers sleep/pause — demo-grade, stated openly.
Q: Why not run the project's own tests? A: No dep installs and no secrets
to project scripts — reported NOT_PERFORMED instead of faked. Q: Revert?
A: New commit restoring blobs (never history rewrite), verified live again.
Q: Cost? A: $0 — Render free, Supabase free, Groq free tier, vanilla JS.
